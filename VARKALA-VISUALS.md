# Varkala visual update

The photographic assets in public/images/varkala and public/journal were extracted from the user-supplied Lux Green.pdf itinerary and converted to WebP. They are a supplied moodboard, not a claim of previous Hodos departures. The villa remains unconfirmed and is labelled accordingly.

The page-curl, tilt, magnifier, zoom and index renderer in public/journal/varkala.html is adapted from the complete Meng To Sketchbook HTML source supplied by the user (ThreeUI, source reference https://threeui.com/landing-pages/meng-to-sketchbook.html). This is an adaptation, not an unmodified copy. Changes include Hodos content, Cormorant Garamond/Noto Sans, local Varkala photo plates, reduced-motion handling, keyboard page-button support, and automatic iframe sizing. Original Singapore illustrations and portfolio content are not used.

The seven journal images are standardized photographic crops. Gallery images retain their source aspect ratios. All trip dates, price, timings, download and enquiry links remain connected to the original Varkala itinerary.
